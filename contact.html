About Us

Founded in 2004, Manly Electronics delivers expert computer repairs with honest, personalised service. Based in Sydney's Northern Beaches, we offer fast, cost-effective solutions tailored to each customer's needs.

Owner Dimitri began repairing electronics while still in school, holds an engineering degree, and has run his business continuously since last century, earning a reputation for practical expertise and reliability.

Prompt & reliable service on-site. Over 20 years of local experience. Fully insured. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
